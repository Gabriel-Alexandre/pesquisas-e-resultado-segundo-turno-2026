 Presidential elections were held in  Cyprus  on 8 February 1998, with a second round on 15 February.   [ 1 ]    The result was a victory for  Glafcos Clerides  of the  Democratic Rally  after he finished as runner-up behind  Georgios Iacovou  (who was supported by  AKEL  and the  Democratic Party )   [ 2 ]    in the first round. Voter turnout was 92% in the first round and 93% in the second.   [ 2 ]
