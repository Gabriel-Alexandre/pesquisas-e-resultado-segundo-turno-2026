This page lists public opinion polls conducted for the [[2022 Colombian presidential election]], the first round of which was held on 29 May. Because no candidate won a majority, a second round was held on 19 June between the top two candidates, [[Gustavo Petro]] and [[Rodolfo Hernández Suárez]].<ref>{{cite news |author1=Holly K. Sonneland |author2=Hope Wilkinson |title=Explainer: Who's Who in Colombia's 2022 Presidential Race |url=https://www.as-coa.org/articles/explainer-whos-who-colombias-2022-presidential-race |access-date=2 May 2022 |work=AS/COA |date=14 March 2022}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Colombia presidential election heads to runoff, with leftist former rebel Petro, populist businessman Hernandez |url=https://abcnews.go.com/International/wireStory/colombia-presidential-election-heads-runoff-leftist-rebel-petro-85058588 |access-date=30 May 2022 |work=ABC News |date=30 March 2022}}</ref>
