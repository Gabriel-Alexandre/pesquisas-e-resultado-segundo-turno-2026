Presidential elections were held in the [[Serbia and Montenegro|Yugoslav]] province of [[Republic of Serbia (1992–2006)|Serbia]] on 29 September 2002, with a second round on 13 October.<ref>[http://www.rik.parlament.gov.rs/arhiva-izbori-za-predsednika-20021.php Elections for the President of the Republic, held on September 29 and October 13, 2002] RIK</ref> However, the result was invalidated because turnout in the second round was less than 50%. [[December 2002 Serbian presidential election|Fresh elections]] were held in December.
