After the previous [[2017 Chilean general election]] in November and December, different polling companies published surveys that tracked voting intention for the '''[[2021 Chilean general election]]''', especially the presidential election. Outgoing president [[Sebastián Piñera]] was not eligible for reelection.

[[2021 Chilean presidential primaries|Official primaries]] were held on 18 July where two coalitions, left-wing [[Apruebo Dignidad]] and right-wing [[Chile Vamos]], chose their respective presidential candidates [[Gabriel Boric]] and [[Sebastián Sichel]].<ref>{{Cite web|date=2021-07-19|title=Gabriel Boric wins Chile presidential primary as protest generation takes centre stage|url=http://www.theguardian.com/world/2021/jul/19/gabriel-boric-chile-president-election|access-date=2021-07-27|website=The Guardian|language=en}}</ref> Centre-left coalition [[New Social Pact]] held their own primary on 21 August 2021 where they selected [[Yasna Provoste]] as their official candidate.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Unidad Constituyente acuerda consulta ciudadana de manera presencial para el 21 de agosto|url=https://www.cnnchile.com/pais/unidad-constituyente-consulta-ciudadana-presencial-agosto_20210725/|access-date=2021-07-27|website=CNN Chile|language=es}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news|title=Con escasa participación, Provoste (DC) se convierte en la abanderada de Unidad Constituyente|url=https://www.latercera.com/la-tercera-domingo/noticia/con-escasa-participacion-provoste-dc-se-convierte-en-la-abanderada-de-unidad-constituyente/I4CHJ6BPIRCLVL7BTM5WN2SYI4/|last=Equipo de Política|date=21 August 2021|access-date=23 August 2021|work=La Tercera|language=es-CL|archive-date=23 August Other candidates were [[José Antonio Kast]] of the [[Christian Social Front]] coalition, [[Franco Parisi]] of the [[Party of the People (Chile)|Party of the People]], [[Marco Enríquez-Ominami]] of the [[Progressive Party (Chile)|Progressive Party]], and [[Eduardo Artés]] of the [[Patriotic Union (Chile)|Patriotic Union]].<ref>{{Cite news|title=Culmina declaración de candidaturas para las Elecciones Generales de noviembre|url=https://www.servel.cl/culmina-declaracion-de-candidaturas-para-las-elecciones-generales-de-noviembre/|date=23 August 2021|access-date=14 August 2021|work=Servicio Electoral The first round of the election took place on 21 November 2021. Since no candidate alone received more than 50% of the votes, a runoff took place on 19 December 2021 between Gabriel Boric and José Antonio Kast.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Conozca todas las fechas del ciclo electoral 2020-2022 – Servicio Electoral de Chile|url=https://www.servel.cl/conozca-todas-las-fechas-del-ciclo-electoral-2020-2022/|access-date=2021-03-23|website=Electoral Service of Chile|date=3 July 2020 |language=es}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|date=2021-11-22|title=Ex-protester, far-right lawmaker to meet in Chilean runoff|url=https://apnews.com/article/business-elections-presidential-elections-south-america-chile-86e72bcd0193207f79479ce9cb726092|access-date=2021-12-08|website=AP NEWS|language=en|archive-date=8 December 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211208181110/https://apnews.com/article/business-elections-presidential-elections-south-america-chile-86e72bcd0193207f79479ce9cb726092|url-status=live}}</ref> Boric won the runoff with 55.87% of the vote and will assume office on 11 March 2022.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.dw.com/en/leftist-boric-set-to-become-new-chile-president-as-kast-concedes-defeat/a-60187979|title=Leftist Boric set to become new Chile president as Kast concedes defeat|publisher=Deutsche Welle|date=2021-12-19|accessdate=2021-12-19|archive-date=19 December 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211219232025/https://www.dw.com/en/leftist-boric-set-to-become-new-chile-president-as-kast-concedes-defeat/a-60187979|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Leftist Gabriel Boric wins Chile presidential election |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/world-latin-america-59715941 |access-date=20 December 2021 |publisher=BBC News |date=20 December 2021 |archive-date=20 December 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211220000309/https://www.bbc.com/news/world-latin-america-59715941 |url-status=live }}</ref>
